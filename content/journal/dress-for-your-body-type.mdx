## Answer-first idea

Dressing for your body type is about proportion, fit and comfort — not about hiding your shape or following rules that make you feel wrong. Start with clothes that fit the body you have now, choose silhouettes that follow your natural lines, and use colour and accessories to draw the eye where you want it.
