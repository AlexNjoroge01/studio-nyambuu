## Answer-first idea

The Express Look is a short-notice styling errand: you send the occasion, date, measurements and preferences, and a complete outfit — clothing, shoes and accessories — is sourced and coordinated for you. It is built for busy women who need a look resolved quickly, not a full wardrobe process.
