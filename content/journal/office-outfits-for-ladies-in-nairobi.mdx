## Answer-first idea

Office outfits in Nairobi do not have to be boring. Start with a tailoring shape that fits, choose one colour or texture per look, and finish with a shoe and accessory that feels like you. A small rotation of jackets, trousers, midi skirts and dresses can cover a whole working week.
